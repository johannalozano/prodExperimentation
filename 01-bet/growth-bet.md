# The Bet · FinWise

> Module 1 · Ignite a PLG Motion. The growth hypothesis and where you're betting, plus the growth-loop visualization.

## Growth hypothesis

FinWise's biggest growth problem is **that trial users are not realizing enough ongoing value from the product to stay engaged and convert to paid**, because **trial-to-paid conversion remains around 2% and only 40% of paid customers are retained after one year**.

_Working notes: The initial hunch was that FinWise needed to simplify onboarding and help users reach value faster. After reviewing the data, the hypothesis was refined: the strongest evidence points to a problem after trial start, where users are not experiencing enough sustained value or stickiness to continue. Evidence: trial-to-paid conversion stays around 2% across the 13-month period, while one-year retention is 40%. Against the data: the dataset does not prove that onboarding itself is the root cause, so the bet is focused on value realization rather than account setup._

_____

## The bet

The highest-leverage experiment is to introduce a **value-focused trial experience** that guides users toward meaningful financial outcomes and reinforces those outcomes throughout the trial.

**IF** we introduce a value-focused experience that helps trial users reach and repeatedly experience FinWise's core value,

**THEN** trial-to-paid conversion will increase by **at least 4 percentage points, from 2% to 6%**,

**BECAUSE** we believe users are starting the trial but are not experiencing enough clear, sustained value to justify paying for the product,

**MEASURED WITH** an A/B test over 4–6 weeks comparing the **current trial experience** against a **value-focused trial experience**, using **trial-to-paid conversion as the primary metric** and **trial disengagement/drop-off as the guardrail metric**.

**Not doing:** We are not prioritizing additional paid acquisition or increasing trial volume, because FinWise is already spending heavily on acquisition and additional spend is no longer driving meaningful growth. Sending more users into the funnel before improving engagement and value realization would pull effort away from the higher-leverage problem.

_____

## Growth loop

**Loop type:** **Collaboration**, because FinWise's main issue appears to be value realization and stickiness rather than acquisition. If users gain more value by working with others inside FinWise, collaboration could deepen engagement and make the product more embedded in their regular financial workflow.

**Loop:**  
Trigger → Shared workflow → More value → Deeper engagement → Expansion + restart → Trigger
