# The Solution · FinWise

> Module 2 · Acquisition & Activation. The onboarding solution and the Aha moment that makes value land.

## Aha moment

FinWise's Aha moment is when a small-business owner connects or imports financial data and immediately sees a clear, personalized view of their cash position, where money is coming from and going, and what needs attention next.

The moment should feel like:

> "My money finally makes sense. I can see what is happening in my business and what I should do next."

For the prototype, the activation signal is reaching and viewing the first personalized cash-flow insight or forecast.

_____

## Onboarding prototype

**Prototype:** https://finwisetestapp.lovable.app

The flow is designed to get a new trial user to value in four screens:

1. **Choose your financial goal**  
   The user selects what they want clarity on first: cash flow, expenses, or getting paid faster.

2. **Bring in your numbers**  
   The user connects business data or uses realistic sample data to avoid unnecessary setup friction.

3. **Generate your financial picture**  
   The user reviews a lightweight summary and clicks **"Show me my financial picture."**

4. **Aha moment**  
   FinWise surfaces a clear cash-flow insight, including projected cash position, expected inflows and outflows, and the financial issue that deserves attention next.

The flow includes a timer to measure **time-to-Aha**, with a target of reaching the value moment in under **2 minutes**.

_____

## Why this activates

The flow is designed around **value realization rather than setup completion**.

Instead of asking users to configure their account, invite teammates, customize dashboards, or complete a long onboarding process, FinWise asks for only the minimum information required to generate a useful financial insight.

A one-question goal selection personalizes the experience early, while the sample-data option lets users experience the product's value before committing to a potentially higher-friction financial integration.

The key change is moving the user quickly from:

**Sign-up → relevant financial context → personalized insight → actionable next step**

This should improve activation because the user experiences FinWise as a tool that does more than store financial data: it helps them understand their business and decide what to do next.

The main friction removed is **unnecessary setup before value**. The hypothesis is that shortening the path to a meaningful financial insight will reduce early disengagement and increase the likelihood that trial users continue using FinWise and eventually convert to paid.
