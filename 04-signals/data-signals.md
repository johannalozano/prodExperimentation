# The Signals · FinWise

> Module 4 · Data & Analytics. The data pattern you found and the indicators you'll steer by.

## The data pattern

FinWise's headline problem is that **trial-to-paid conversion is stuck at roughly 2%**, but the underlying usage data shows that the issue is not simply whether users access deeper product features.

Across the 13-month period:

- Trial-to-paid conversion shows a clear **Ceiling**, remaining almost flat around 2%.
- Financial Modeling Feature Usage shows a **Cliff / volatile pattern**, with sharp rises and falls across months.
- Get Started Import Feature Usage shows a **Ceiling**, tending to remain around the 40–45% range.
- Average Session Duration shows a **Cliff**, dropping sharply from 12.11 minutes in June 2024 to 6.99 in July and 5.14 in August.
- Average Sessions Per User shows a **Slow Leak** early in the period, falling from 9 to 3 sessions per user before later recovering.

When Financial Modeling Feature Usage was compared with Trial-to-Paid Conversion, the relationship was weak, with a Pearson correlation of approximately **0.24**.

This revises the original hypothesis slightly: **getting users to access the modeling feature is not, by itself, enough to drive conversion**. The deeper problem appears to be whether users reach meaningful value from that experience quickly and consistently enough for it to influence their decision to stay and pay.

## Leading indicators

**Leading 1 — % of trial users who import data and reach their first modeling output**

This measures how effectively users progress from starting a trial to reaching the behavior most closely associated with FinWise's Aha moment.

The available dataset does not contain this exact combined event, so **Financial Modeling Feature Usage %** was used as the closest available proxy for the correlation test.

**Leading 2 — Median time to first modeling output**

This measures how quickly users reach the Aha moment. A shorter time-to-value should reduce early disengagement and increase the likelihood that users experience FinWise's core value during the trial.

Together, these indicators measure both:

- **Whether users reach value**
- **How quickly they reach it**

_____

## Lagging indicators

**Primary lagging indicator — Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate**

This is the ultimate proof that improvements in value realization are translating into commercial growth. It is lagging because it only becomes visible after users have progressed through enough of the trial to make a purchase decision.

Additional lagging indicators that would help validate the strategy over a longer horizon include:

- **Paid-customer retention**
- **Annual churn**
- **Revenue / ARR growth**

These metrics confirm whether improvements in activation and engagement ultimately produce durable business outcomes.
