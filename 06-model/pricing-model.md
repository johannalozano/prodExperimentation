# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** **Stage 2 · Revenue Expansion**, because FinWise has already reached product-market fit and $10M ARR, but monetization remains weak with only 2% of trial users converting to paid and just 40% of paid users retained after one year.

**Model:** **Freemium through the existing reverse-trial motion**, because FinWise delivers value continuously and users need enough time to experience meaningful financial insights before being asked to pay. A usable free tier can preserve adoption and habit, while paid packaging captures deeper and recurring value.

_____

## Recommendation

Redesign the free/paid boundary around **value realization**. Allow users to import financial data and reach their first meaningful financial insight or modeling output before the paid boundary. Gate the recurring, higher-value capabilities — such as ongoing forecasting, deeper financial modeling, weekly financial-health monitoring, and collaboration — behind the paid tier.

This lets FinWise prove its value before asking users to upgrade, while still monetizing the workflows that become more valuable as users rely on the product over time.

**The pricing bet:** **Packaging and paywall placement matched to realized value** — users should experience their first meaningful financial insight for free, then pay to make that value recurring, deeper, and embedded in their financial workflow.

_____

## Model signal

The key signals are the Module 4 leading indicators:

- **% of trial users who import data and reach their first modeling output**
- **Median time to first modeling output**

If users reach the Aha moment more often and faster, and then convert at a higher rate when recurring value is gated, that would indicate the free/paid boundary is positioned more effectively.

The main lagging proof is **trial-to-paid conversion rate**. If conversion improves without worsening retention, the pricing model is likely capturing value more effectively.
